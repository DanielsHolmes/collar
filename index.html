Procore And NetSuite Both Matter.
The Problem Is When They Do Not Talk In Context.

Construction companies often depend on both Procore and NetSuite. Procore may support project operations, field workflows, commitments, change orders, daily reports, punch lists, budgets, and job-site communication. NetSuite may manage the financial side, including billing, invoicing, approvals, revenue recognition, AP, AR, reporting, and accounting controls. Both systems have a real job to do. The issue is the gap between them.

Keep Procore In The Field
And NetSuite In Financial Control

The point is not to make contractors choose between Procore and NetSuite. For many construction companies, Procore is already valuable for field teams and project activity. NetSuite is already valuable for finance, billing, reporting, approvals, and ERP control.

The better model is coexistence. BlueCollar's Procore Connector lets contractors keep Procore in place for the workflows it already supports while creating a cleaner connection to the NetSuite financial backbone.

Approval-Gated Sync
Gives Accounting Control

Not every record should move into Procore the moment it exists in NetSuite. Finance teams need control over what becomes visible to project teams, especially when financial records are still being reviewed, adjusted, or approved.

BlueCollar's Procore Connector supports approval-gated sync. Approved records in NetSuite can sync to Procore, while unapproved records remain inside NetSuite. That gives accounting teams a stronger control point.

  • ✓ Finance reviews and approves records in NetSuite
  • ✓ Approved financial data syncs to Procore
  • ✓ Project teams gain visibility into approved records
  • ✓ Unapproved data remains outside Procore until ready
