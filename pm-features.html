Manage Submittals With Structure, Status,
And Financial Context

Submittals are more than documents. They confirm that materials, drawings, specs, product data, equipment, or other project details meet the requirements before purchase or installation.

Submittal List View

Track submittal number, CSI spec section, title, type, revision, status, ball-in-court, assignee, manager, and due date in one structured view.

Aging Visibility

Days open or closed at a glance so teams can quickly spot aging items and follow up before delays compound.

Attached Files & Communication

Files and communication tied to the project record instead of scattered across email threads or separate drives.

Organize Related Work
With Submittal Packages

Large projects rarely move one document at a time. Submittals often belong to a larger scope, phase, trade, spec section, or deliverable. Without a better way to group them, teams can lose the bigger picture and spend too much time managing individual records in isolation.

BlueCollar supports submittal packages, allowing related items to be grouped under a common workflow. This helps project teams organize documentation in a way that matches how construction work actually happens. A package can make it easier to manage approvals across trades, phases, milestones, or specification areas.

Connect Submittal Line Items
To Cost Visibility

Many construction document tools stop at status tracking. They can show whether a submittal is open, closed, submitted, or approved. That helps, but it does not always answer the financial questions that matter to contractors.

BlueCollar adds line-item details to submittals, giving teams a clearer view of the materials, quantities, manufacturers, costs, delivery details, and approval information associated with each record.

Keep Revisions Clean
And Easy To Trace

Construction teams need to know which version is current. A missed revision can create confusion in the field, slow down approvals, or lead to decisions based on outdated information.

BlueCollar supports revision history for submittals, including Rev 0, Rev 1, Rev 2, and beyond. Once a new revision is created, older revisions become read-only, helping teams preserve the record without creating confusion about which version to act on.

Send Submittals From NetSuite

Track RFIs
Before Answers Get Lost

RFIs are where uncertainty becomes official. Field teams, project managers, subcontractors, or other stakeholders need a clear path to ask for clarification on drawings, specs, missing details, field conditions, conflicts, or project decisions.

Without a structured RFI workflow, those decisions often get buried in email threads, meeting notes, or informal conversations. BlueCollar PM Features give contractors a structured RFI workflow with assignee, manager, due date, status, files, and project record visibility.

Capture Job-Site Context
With Daily Reports

Daily reports help contractors capture what happened on the job site. Labor, weather, progress, delays, issues, notes, and project activity all become part of the operating record.

When daily reports live outside the project and financial environment, the information can be harder to use later. Teams may know what happened on-site, but finance, operations, and leadership may not have a clear way to link that activity to project performance.

BlueCollar Daily Reports are designed to bring job-site records into the BlueCollar and NetSuite environment, giving contractors a stronger link between field activity and operational visibility.
