Three ways BlueCollar NetSuite Managed Services keeps your system running and your team stronger.

We Run It

Day-to-day system support should not feel like a help desk reading from a script. Your setup has history, customizations, workflows, reporting logic, role permissions, integrations, saved searches, scripts, and business rules that affect how the company actually works.

BlueCollar NetSuite Managed Services helps administer and support the system with people who understand both NetSuite and construction operations. Support is not limited to "what button did you click?" It includes how the system is supposed to support billing, job cost visibility, WIP, retainage, reporting, field workflows, approvals, and month-end close.

  • ✓NetSuite administration and configuration support
  • ✓BlueCollar Projects and extension support
  • ✓NetSuite Field Service Management support
  • ✓Workflow, saved search, and reporting review
  • ✓Role, permission, and access review
  • ✓Support for ongoing questions and system issues
  • ✓Process cleanup when the system is being worked around
  • ✓Upgrade protection before new releases reach production

NetSuite releases and module updates do not wait for your team to have extra time. We help test your setup before changes reach production, so you are not finding out after the fact that a billing process, workflow, saved search, script, or SuiteApp has been affected.

Upgrade protection can include sandbox testing, impact review of custom scripts, workflows, saved searches, and third-party SuiteApps, a regression checklist tailored to your configuration, and a go/no-go call with a remediation plan.

We Push It Forward

A support relationship should not only answer your questions. It should help surface what you have not had time to see.

BlueCollar NetSuite Managed Services includes roadmap planning and proactive review. We bring you recommendations: what to fix, what to turn on, what to stop paying for, what to document, what to simplify, and what should be addressed before the next quarter creates more friction.

Quarterly reviews can help your team understand:

  • →Which reports no longer match how the business works
  • →Which workflows are creating unnecessary manual effort
  • →Which modules are owned but not being used well
  • →Which spreadsheet workarounds should be moved back into the system
  • →Which customizations are creating upgrade risk
  • →Which teams need more training or documentation
  • →Which process gaps are being blamed on the software

Sometimes the answer is not more software. Sometimes the answer is changing how month-end close works, turning on something you already own, removing a customization that makes every upgrade painful, or training the team to use what was already built.

That kind of candor is the point. The roadmap should help your business, not just create more billable work.

We Staff It

Construction accounting talent is hard to find. A general controller may be strong, but still needs months to understand WIP, retainage, AIA pay applications, percentage-of-completion, certified payroll, change order flow, and the way those pieces affect reporting and project financials.

BlueCollar can help fill capacity gaps with construction-fluent accounting and system talent. This is not a staffing-agency model where resumes are thrown over the wall and the person is left on an island. The value is embedded expertise backed by the BlueCollar bench.

Depending on confirmed availability and fit, support may include roles such as:

  • ✓Controller support
  • ✓Project accountant support
  • ✓Billing and AR support
  • ✓Payroll support
  • ✓NetSuite system administrator support

The person stepping in already understands the construction context and the system environment we support. They can help run the work, train the people around them, document what they build, and hand work back when it makes sense.
