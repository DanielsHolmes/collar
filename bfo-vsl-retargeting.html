If You’re Still Thinking About BFO,
One of These Is Probably Still True.

Contractors who re-engage with BlueCollar usually do so because the fundamental back-office friction hasn't gone away on its own.

Condition 01

You are considering hiring a Controller or Finance Manager.

You know leadership needs strategic financial ownership, but hiring one full-time individual in today's construction market carries high cost, long recruiting cycles, and potential key-person dependency.

Condition 02

WIP is difficult to reconcile or does not tie out consistently.

Project inputs are delayed, changes do not get reflected on time, and monthly work-in-progress numbers feel like an end-of-month fire drill instead of an orderly management control.

Condition 03

Your current accounting system or workflow is reaching its limits.

Spreadsheet workarounds, disconnected PM software, and entry-level accounting tools can no longer keep up with project volume and reporting requirements.

Condition 04

The Owner / President is still the escalation point for too many financial issues.

Instead of spending time on business development, key relationships, and execution, the Owner is bogged down signing off on operational details and resolving back-office discrepancies.

Condition 05

Project growth is increasing faster than the back office supporting it.

Top-line contract volume is up, more PMs are running jobs in the field, but finance visibility is lagging behind—putting cash flow and job profitability at risk.

Those are not five separate problems. They often point to the same question: does the business have the right system, the right people, and clear ownership behind construction financial operations?

Software + People.
One Operating Model.

The BFO model replaces disjointed point solutions with a single, synchronized financial operating layer.

BFO Layer 01

Financial Platform

The structure for project financial data, job costing, WIP calculations, and reporting. Powered by NetSuite's robust construction engine, configured for real field-to-office accountability.

BFO Layer 02

Experienced Construction-Finance Personnel

Controller-level review, follow-through, and operational judgment. Experienced specialists who understand retainage, AIA billing, change order management, and trade contractor operations.

BFO Layer 03

Recurring Cadence

A repeatable rhythm for PM / finance review, WIP, reporting, open items, and accountability. Moving your finance function from chaotic reaction to structured, forward-looking operational cadence.

The goal is not more accounting activity. It is a back office that can keep up with the projects and gives leadership a clearer operating owner.
WIP & Job Cost Integrity

When the Numbers Don’t Match the Job, the Operating Process Deserves a Look.

Reliable WIP depends on the calculation environment, current project inputs, PM context, and someone accountable for reviewing and reconciling the information.

BFO is designed to bring those pieces together so WIP and job-cost review become a recurring operating process, not a last-minute fire drill.
